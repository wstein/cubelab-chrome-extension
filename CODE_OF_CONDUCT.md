# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, accept criticism of your work gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and the cubing community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, pull requests and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report problems privately through GitHub: open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/wstein/cubelab-chrome-extension/security/advisories/new) or contact [@wstein](https://github.com/wstein) directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
